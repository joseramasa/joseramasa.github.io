**[NanophotonicsLab — Browser-Based Nanophotonic Simulation](https://nanophotonicslab.com/)**

A no-install browser toolkit for nanophotonic simulation and teaching. Interactive tools for light-matter interactions at the nanoscale — from plasmonics and photonic crystals to quantum optics.

### Tools

- **Mie Scattering** — scattering cross-sections for spherical particles
- **Plasmonic Nanoparticles** — optical response of metallic nanostructures
- **Cylinder** — scattering and field distributions for cylindrical geometries
- **Photothermal** — heating effects in nanostructures under illumination
- **Electron** — electron-beam interaction tools
- **Photon** — photon statistics and quantum optics simulators
- **BPM** — Beam Propagation Method for waveguide analysis
- **Laser** — laser cavity and beam calculators
- **RCWA** — Rigorous Coupled-Wave Analysis for periodic structures
- **Units** — quick unit converter for photonics

### Features

- Runs entirely in the browser — no installation needed
- Dark/light theme
- Offline support via service worker
