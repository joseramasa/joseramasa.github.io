[Quside](https://quside.com) released [**quside-curand**](https://quside.com/quside-brings-verifiable-quantum-entropy-to-nvidia-cuda-workflows-with-quside-curand/), a cuRAND-style host API that brings verifiable, locally generated entropy to C++ and NVIDIA CUDA workflows. Entropy goes where provenance matters (seeds, keys, critical values); bulk generation stays on the GPU.
